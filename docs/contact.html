FAQ

Common Questions

How do I track my order?
Go to your Dashboard → Orders and click "Track" next to your order. You'll get real-time updates from our delivery partners.
What is the return policy?
You can return eligible items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Initiate returns from your Orders page.
How do I pay for my order?
We accept GCash, Maya, credit/debit cards, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible areas.
Is it safe to buy on PickSell?
Yes! All sellers are verified and every purchase is covered by our Buyer Protection Program. We hold payment until you confirm receipt.
How do I become a seller?
Register for a free account, go to your Dashboard, and click "Start Selling". Setup takes less than 5 minutes.
